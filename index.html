02COMPLEMENTARY SENSING

No single sensor sees the entire belt.

Different defects produce different signatures.

SURFACE

Industrial camera

OPTICAL FIELD OF VIEW
  • Cracks, tears & holes
  • Abrasion & foreign objects
  • Visible splice damage
GEOMETRY

Laser 3D profiler

SURFACE HEIGHT PROFILE
  • Wear, grooves & deformation
  • Thickness / shape variation
  • Edge damage & splice geometry
INTERNAL

MFL / Hall array

CONCEPTUAL FLUX LEAKAGE
  • Broken & displaced steel cords
  • Corrosion & internal abnormalities
  • Internal splice / cord condition
